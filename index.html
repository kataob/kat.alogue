Profile

I am a power systems engineer with five years of experience across renewable energy project development, grid integration, research, and optimization-based design of renewable power plants. Through a PhD at the Technical University of Denmark, an applied research stay at TotalEnergies, and prior project development work at RP Global, I learned to combine feasibility studies, techno-economic modeling, and owner-side technical support to bridge optimized system design with real-world project delivery. I am excited to face the ongoing challenges of the power system, and support their part in shaping a sustainable future in this rapidly developing world.
