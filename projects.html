Mini Projects

A few smaller, self-directed explorations outside of my core research — places where I get to poke at an idea just because it's interesting.

TopoFun

A playground for applying FunSearch — DeepMind's LLM-guided evolutionary search over code — to topology optimization problems. The idea is to see whether a language model can evolve genuinely new, high-performing update rules the way it already has for open problems in pure mathematics.

the-part-and-the-whole

A city is both a self-contained energy system and a subsystem of a much larger national grid. This project digs into that tension — how urban and national energy models talk past (and sometimes contradict) each other — and what changes when a city is treated as a part rather than a whole.
